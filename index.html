Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book your cleaning.

What areas does LuHe Carpet Cleaning serve?

We're based in Shrewsbury, MA and serve Worcester, Northborough, Westborough, Marlborough, Natick, Bellingham, and the surrounding MetroWest and Central Massachusetts area. If you're not sure whether your town is covered, send us a message and we'll let you know.

What services do you offer?

We specialize in carpet, sofa and upholstery, mattress, area rug, and chair cleaning — everything your family sits, sleeps, and walks on. Each service uses professional-grade equipment and safe cleaning solutions. Have a specific request? Call us and we'll let you know how we can help.

How do I get a free quote?

Call or text (508) 736-8397, email luhecleaning@gmail.com, or fill out the quote form on this page. We respond as quickly as we can during business hours.

How much does carpet or sofa cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the area and the condition of the fabric. The fastest way to get a clear price is to send a few photos with your quote request — we'll quote you back before any work starts.

Do you bring everything you need?

Yes. We arrive with the equipment and cleaning solutions for the job, treat the carpet or upholstery on site, and leave it ready for you to use.
